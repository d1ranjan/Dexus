# Dexus Creator and Ownership Attribution

## Public product identity

The public-facing Dexus product is presented as **Dexus**, with the tagline **“Connect your thoughts.”** Creator attribution appears as **“Created by Dipanshu Ranjan”** and the standard footer is **“Dexus © 2026 Dipanshu Ranjan.”**

This attribution identifies Dipanshu Ranjan as the creator and owner of the Dexus application to the extent permitted by the relevant platform and service terms. It does not claim ownership of third-party infrastructure, libraries, AI models, or other services used by the product.

## Brand-safety boundary

Editable user-facing Dexus copy and metadata should not contain unnecessary builder references, promotional badges, or builder-brand copy. Required technical runtime integrations, storage domains, platform-level attribution, and service identifiers remain unchanged where removal could impair the product or violate platform requirements.

## Ownership and roles

Dexus retains its existing server-enforced owner/admin architecture. The configured owner identity receives the highest existing application-level `admin` role only during server-side user upsert. There is no user-facing self-promotion flow. Normal users cannot request or assign administrator status.
